# Dispatch matrix routing-v1: review tables

Generated by `python scripts/intake/matrix_tool.py record`; do not edit. Read these tables, then sign `approval.json` (status, name, date). What you approve is the matrix file and every cell below: **which sensors a question gets in full, and which knowledge it looks in.**

## How to read the cells

- A sensor is written as its number: `1` is MCD1. **ALWAYS** is the trader type's primary sensor and every warning sensor (ADR-041); **EXTRA** is what the intent's row adds. A cell reads `ALWAYS + EXTRA-only`: `1 3 + 2` means MCD1 and MCD3 are always there and MCD2 comes from the intent's rule. `-` is none.
- **Primary sensor**: the sensor whose registry rung is `primary_structure` for the trader type. **Warning**: a VALID or CAUTIONARY sensor that says STAND_ASIDE, or whose LONG or SHORT is the opposite of a LONG or SHORT synthesis (NEUTRAL is not a warning; a synthesis that stands aside has nothing to oppose).
- A sensor is given in full only if it is live and has a reading for the cycle. In these 16 cycles every sensor that has a reading is live.

## Readings of the rules to confirm

These are the decisions D2 and D10 as the code reads them. Say if any is wrong.

1. **Always** applies to every intent except Report 2 and Out of scope (they carry no readings at all). "Board only" for Risk means no *extra* sensor: the primary sensor and the warnings are still in full.
2. **Entry timing** adds the sensors named as sources of the zones of the trader's reading. With no zones it adds none.
3. **Exit and targets** adds a sensor if any of its levels is strictly beyond the entry on the target side. The entry is the first zone's reference price, or the close of the reading when there is no zone; the target side is above for a LONG and below for a SHORT; a synthesis with no direction adds none.
4. **Explain** with several owners gives the primary sensor and the sensor that reads only the timeframe named. The sensor that reads both timeframes (MCD3) is never "the sensor of the timeframe".
5. **The instant prompts** add the sensors that read the channel of the label's timeframe: M5 gives MCD2 and MCD3, M15 gives MCD1 and MCD3.
6. **A primary sensor that is INVALID or STALE** is still given in full (its envelope says why it is unavailable). One with no reading at all (ABSENT) cannot be.
7. **Knowledge** is the domains of the row; the domain-D2 playbooks are searched only for the sensors in M*.
8. **Not here**: MCD0 is a gate, not a line on the board; its defects show as reasons on the channel sensors.
