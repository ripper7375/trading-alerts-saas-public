## 7. Problems and surprises the next session must know

1. **The instruction's formula and the floors.** `min(T_EDT − 1, cap)` is right for the cap (MCD2: 288), but both MCDs also have a floor (48 and 96) that today lengthens a short window past the channel. Removing that overreach by shrinking the window removes the floor too, and no existing document says that is wanted: MCD1's spec says the micro window is "never fewer than 96 bars" so that 2 to 3 hours of news cannot pass for a breakout. The sections write the floor as a minimum and ask (Q1). MCD1 has no cap: its target `max(96, 5% of T_EDT)` plays that role, and for `T_EDT` of 97 or more it is already at most `T_EDT − 1`, so with the floor kept the MCD1 change is **only the reason code** of a reading that is INVALID before and after (`DISCONTINUITY` to `INSUFFICIENT_BARS`).
2. **Three MCD2 tests change.** `test_the_window_is_between_48_and_288_bars`, `test_the_bar_count_must_reach_the_window`, and the 100 and 60 cases of `test_t_edt_falls_back_in_order_and_ends_at_288`. They build synthetic bundles with bands on every row, which a real channel never has (it has `T_EDT − 1` rows). The tests need a realistic short-channel generator. The standard's "tests pass unchanged" for a PATCH is therefore not literally met for MCD2; it is met for MCD1 (no test below `T_EDT` 545). The fixtures, `mcd2_output.json` and `mcd1_output.json` do not move.
3. **When `T_EDT` is unknown** the fallback stays (MCD2 288, MCD1 96): there is nothing to subtract. This is carried over from each spec's Q2 and is not a new decision.
4. **MCD3 is not affected in its results,** and is only affected in live data in one way: a short M5 channel no longer reaches it as `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE:MCD2` because of this defect. MCD3 reads status, trend and angle, not reason codes, so the MCD1 reason-code change is invisible to it. With Q1 answered as recommended, MCD2 and MCD3 agree that an M5 channel with `T_EDT` of 48 or less is too short (MCD3: `N_nest` below 48).
5. **The fit-window question is still open** (does the statistics fit use the forming bar; MQL5 source needed). This patch does not depend on it; the parameter in Q2 turns a different answer into a one-value change (a MINOR change then).
6. **Third copy of a helper:** the "T_EDT from the first number among the fallback fields" logic now exists in MCD1, MCD2 and MCD3. Moving it into the kit would be a kit change for Davin to schedule; not proposed here.
7. The scratch probe (`p7_probe.py`) is in the session scratchpad, outside the repository. It only patches the evaluators in memory.
8. Line endings: the repository files are LF on disk and at HEAD; every edit here was made as LF and verified by counting CR bytes (the known trap, [environment-gotchas](../../.claude/architecture/environment-gotchas.md)). Every `git` call warns "LF will be replaced by CRLF" (`core.autocrlf=true`).
