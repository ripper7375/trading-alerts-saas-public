## 2. Deviations from the order (and why)

Live code and evidence won over the order's wording where they disagreed (non-negotiable 7). Each is small and reversible; please confirm or say which to undo.

1. **The exporter is in `mq5/`, not in the folder above it.** Every exporter and indicator `.mq5` lives in `mq5/` (the order named `v2_29_data_pipeline_architecture/` itself, where only the legacy collector EA sits). Move it if you compile from somewhere else.
2. **`captured_at` is UTC from `TimeTradeServer() − the hour-rounded offset`, not raw `TimeCurrent()`.** `TimeCurrent()` is the last tick's _server_ time: hours off UTC and stalled on a quiet market. The collector's own docstring (`export_file_mtime`), the calendar exporter and blueprint §7.1 all record that this exact mistake mis-stamped every pipeline row for two years. A test (`test_the_exporter_stamps_utc_never_raw_server_time`) pins it.
3. **`typical_spread` is the median of `SYMBOL_SPREAD` samples, not one reading.** The order lists `SYMBOL_SPREAD`; §6.9 says "median of `SYMBOL_SPREAD` per cycle". One sample per minute over a rolling 24 hours, taken only while the last quote is under 2 minutes old (a closed market adds nothing), no export until 30 samples. All five numbers are EA inputs. A single reading would also have made the "on change" rule fire every cycle.
4. **The exporter is an EA on a timer, not a script.** A script runs once and cannot sample. It follows `EconomicCalendarExport_v2_29.mq5`.
5. **The file is tab-separated text, not CSV or JSON.** It is what the collector parses everywhere else, by column name, with the same UTF-8 and rename handling.
6. **A guard the order did not ask for:** the exporter refuses to start on a chart whose symbol does not begin with `InpSymbol` (default `XAUUSD`), and the collector refuses any row whose symbol is not `XAUUSD`. Figures from the wrong chart (EURUSD: contract size 100000) would be stored as gold's and used to size a lot.
7. **What "changed or daily" means, concretely:** a **contract figure** changed (the ten that are not `captured_at`, `symbol` or `typical_spread`), or the newest row is 24 hours old. A wandering spread alone appends nothing (it would be a row per cycle). Only a strictly newer `captured_at` can append, so a re-read, and an older file from a standby terminal after a promote, are no-ops. A file dated more than 600 s ahead of the VPS clock is refused.
8. **The DTO is written by hand, not generated.** The generator emits only type, enum and required, and these figures size a lot (a contract size of 0 must be a 400). A test runs one corpus (more than 150 cases, both verdicts) through the schema (Ajv) and through the DTO and fails on any disagreement. The contract also constrains `symbol` (`^[A-Za-z0-9._-]{1,32}$`: it becomes a job id and a database key) and bounds the three integers at 2147483647 (a real Postgres confirmed the column is 32 bits).
9. **`terminal_id` on this lane is the MT5 terminal's folder name** (`C:/MT5-A/MQL5/Files` gives `MT5-A`), taken by the collector, not the push worker's sender id `push_worker_v5` that the other lanes carry in that field. Prisma's own column comment says "when the terminal was read", and a promote can move the collector to a terminal on another account.
10. **The schema mirror is in `railway-gateway/src/symbol-specs/`, not `src/cycle/`** (nothing about it is a cycle).

### How a row gets from the terminal to the table

The EA samples the spread every 60 s (only while quotes are live) and every 300 s writes `SymbolSpecs_XAUUSD.txt` with the broker's figures and the median. Each collector cycle reads it, and appends a `symbol_specs` row only if it is news (deviation 7), tagged with the terminal's folder. The push worker sends unsynced rows, oldest first, to `/api/v1/symbol-specs`. The gateway validates the whole batch (one bad element refuses it all), queues one job per element keyed `<symbol>_<captured_at>`, and the processor records the row with the next per-symbol version, or acknowledges a repeat without a second row. Engine 4 (Section 6, later) will read the newest row by `captured_at` and put its `version` in the consent record.

## 7. Decisions needed from Davin

None blocks part 9. Six, each with a recommendation:

1. **The deviations in section 2.** Recommend keep all ten. The ones that change behaviour you might care about: 2 (UTC, not raw `TimeCurrent()`), 3 (a 24 h median) and 7 (what counts as news).
2. **The median's window and warm-up** (24 h, one sample a minute, 30 samples first, quotes under 2 minutes old). They are EA inputs, so changing them needs no code. A restart of the EA restarts the window. Recommend keep.
3. **The three extra columns from part 2 (`point`, `digits`, `swap_mode`) are now required by the contract.** Still open from part 2: recommend keep; without them Engine 4 cannot turn a spread in points into a price or read the swap.
4. **Do you want `getSymbolSpecByVersion(symbol, version)` now?** The consent record names a `version`, and a later trace will want the figures it meant. It is five lines and not in the order. Recommend add it when Section 6 is built.

## 8. Problems and surprises the next session must know

1. **The exporter has never been compiled or run.** I read it line by line against the calendar exporter it copies from and tested what it must contain, but a MetaEditor error, or a property that behaves differently on your broker (`SYMBOL_TIME` as the quote clock, `SYMBOL_SWAP_MODE` values), would only show on the first compile and the first export.
2. **A gateway deployed before the Part 2 migration loses rows.** The endpoint answers 200 on enqueue; a job that then fails for a missing table is gone after its 3 attempts, and the push worker has already stamped the row. The daily refresh would record the figures again within 24 hours, but do not rely on it: apply the migration first.
3. **The exporter must be on both A and B**, or promoting to the one without it stops the refresh with no error (documented in the promote runbook). It also needs about 30 minutes to warm up after being attached, so attach the standby well before a promote.
4. **A push worker that runs before the gateway has the endpoint** gets a 404 on every pass (every 30 seconds), logs it, and counts `send_attempts` up. Nothing else is affected and nothing is quarantined.
5. **`test_extended_statistics.py` already fails 15 checks at HEAD** (statistics parsing against the real captures; I ran it against the HEAD collector to prove it). Not touched.
6. **The generated DTOs declare less than their schemas.** `economic-event.dto.ts` does not enforce its schema's `pattern` on `value_id` or its `minimum` on `captured_at`, because the generator emits only type, enum and required. Reported, not changed (out of scope).
7. **Versions are in the order the gateway records rows, not the order observed.** A late older observation gets a higher version than a newer one that came first, so "the latest" is decided by `captured_at` (the reader does this), never by the highest version.
8. **Two equivalent survivors were removed rather than excused** (a redundant `@IsString()` beside `@Matches`, and an empty-field check that `int('')` and `float('')` already make). Both are noted in the code.
9. **No UI text was added**, so the i18n rules do not apply.
