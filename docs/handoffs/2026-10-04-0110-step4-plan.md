## 3. Design in one picture

- **Where.** Python, a new package `mcd_worker/synthesis/`, called by `Worker.run_cycle` right after the MCDs, in the same subprocess call (ADR-027; step 3's settled Q2 (a): one implementation, no port). No new dependency: prices use the standard library's `decimal`.
- **In.** The final envelopes after MCD0 inheritance, the bundle (last closed M5 close, `data_status`, `retuning`, and the new `context_levels`), the rules file, the zone parameters.
- **Out.** An additive `synthesis` section of `mcd-cycle-result/1`: two `syn-output/1` readings (`DAY_TRADER`, `SCALPER`) and the `entry_zones` of each. The reading is byte-stable canonical JSON like an envelope.
- **Rules.** `rules/draft-1.yaml`: ordered, versioned, first match wins (ADR-026). Each row holds its id, profile, conditions, result, archetype, trend relation and the English reason templates. 3.4 is transcribed as it stands; where it is silent my reading is written down and listed in D1 for you to edit. Each edit is a new rules version and a decision-log entry. Zone constants (10%, $0.50, $13, at most 5) live in `zone_params.yaml` with value, unit, boundary and why, like MCD parameters.
- **Stored.** The gateway writes the readings and zones in the same transaction as the cycle's `mcd_outputs` rows (D9). Append-only, unique per cycle and profile.
- **Replay.** The step 3 path: stored bundle, runner, byte-for-byte comparison, now including SYN and zones.
- **Off by default.** SYN flag `off`; nothing deployed; no migration applied.
