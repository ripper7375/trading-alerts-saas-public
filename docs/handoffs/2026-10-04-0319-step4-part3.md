The worker refuses to start on a bad `SYN`, on `SYN` above its sensors, or on a flag that needs a synthesizer when there is none. The rules and the zone parameters are read only when `SYN` is `shadow` or `live`.
- **The runner calls synthesis** (`cycle_runner.py`, new `synthesis/cycle.py`): after the last MCD, in the same call, the Day Trader and Scalper readings and their zones are made from the final (post-inheritance) envelopes the flag lets it see, the bundle's closed M5 bars and `context_levels`. A reading that fails its guard is not saved and its zones are dropped; zones that fail `zone_problems` are dropped and the reading names none; both are listed in `guard_problems`. **An exception in synthesis ends in `synthesis.error = "SYNTHESIS_ERROR"`, no SYN readings and a logged error: never a changed or missing MCD row.** With `SYN` off a result has no `synthesis` key and no `synthesis_ms`: it is byte for byte what it was.
