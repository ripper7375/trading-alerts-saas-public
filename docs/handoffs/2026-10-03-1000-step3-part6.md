## 6. Decisions needed from Davin, with the options seen

None blocks part 7. Decisions 1 to 3 shape what B4 measures and are best settled before it; 4 is a record.

1. **An outcome exists only when every M5 bar of the window is there.** Your decision says 24 and 144 M5 bars; it does not say what a market closure does. Built: the reference bar, the horizon bar and every bar between must exist; a daily break, a weekend or a hole in the history leaves the occurrence without an outcome at that horizon and it is **not counted in n** (the engine returns the counts by reason: `NO_REFERENCE_BAR`, `NO_HORIZON_BAR`, `GAP_IN_WINDOW`). It is never stretched to "the 24th bar later", which would be a 3-hour window across a one-hour break. Options: **keep (recommended; look at the counts at B4)**; or require only the two end bars and let a closure inside the window pass (a closure moves no price, and the reopening bar's own high and low hold the gap, but a hole from a collector failure would pass too); or a trading-time horizon (needs a market-hours calendar). Cost of keeping: states that happen in the 12 hours before a break or a weekend are under-represented at 12 h; nothing is mis-measured.
2. **n counts occurrences (cycles), not independent episodes.** ADR-022 says "n >= 30 per state and horizon". With a cycle every 5 minutes, 30 consecutive cycles in one state are 2.5 hours of one episode, and their forward windows overlap almost completely, so n = 30 can be a single observation with 30 copies. The engine counts every occurrence the caller passes. Options: **decide before B4**: keep ADR-022 as written (n counts cycles) and say so in `series_notes`; or count an episode once (a run of consecutive cycles in one state is one occurrence, taken at its first cycle), which the caller can do without any change here; or thin the sample. A different rule is a new ADR that names ADR-022. I recommend counting episodes, but it is your call and it changes how many states reach 30.
3. **Which readings count as occurrences.** The engine takes what it is given. On all three real fixtures MCD0 flags both timeframes, so every channel MCD is CAUTIONARY (plan S3); "VALID only" would leave MCD1 to MCD3 with almost no occurrences, and counting CAUTIONARY readings counts states read through a flagged channel. A reading made while RETUNING is enforced is the same question. Yours before B4.
4. **Record the arithmetic.** Your Q9 (b) answers are in `history/resolved-waiting-on.md`, in `outcomes.py` and in this report, but not in `STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md` section 2.8 or an ADR. Options: **a short addition to section 2.8 (recommended, no new decision: it defines what ADR-022's "forward move" and "adverse excursion" are)**, or an ADR. I did not edit the architecture.
5. **Smaller, builder's calls, listed so you can overrule them:** `PROVISIONAL` answers carry a reason (`NO_HISTORY`, `BELOW_MIN_SAMPLE`, `ROW_UNUSABLE`) and an English sentence for the prompt (the pivot of the 16 languages); a series never seen is `NO_HISTORY` with n = 0, the same as no row; one state with two biases in a series stops the engine with an error (the worker's guard already makes a state's bias equal to its register's, `guards.py` REGISTER, so this should never happen inside one `MAJOR.MINOR`: it would be a register change shipped as a PATCH); the golden rows live in `mcd_worker/tests/data/` because a test pins `mcd_worker/fixtures/` to slot-named files.
