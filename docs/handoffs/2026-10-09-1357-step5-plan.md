## 3. How §6.5's stop options reach the secondary levels (the seam)

6.5 builds the stop options from "each structural invalidation level at or beyond Min SLD" (its example: below M5 LOEDT and below M15 UOEDT). 3.9 hands step 5 one `invalidation_price` and one `next_opposing_price` per zone, and `entry_zones` stores exactly one of each (`levels` holds only `source_levels`, `confluence_levels`, `invalidation_level` and `next_opposing_level`). The other levels are not in the zone row. They are in two stored places, and these are exactly the two lists that `mcd_worker/synthesis/cycle.py:141` hands to the zone builder (`levels_from_readings(readings) + sr_levels_from_context(inputs.context_levels)`):

| Levels                                                                                                        | Stored in                                                                                                                                                                               | Retention                              | Check at read time                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Channel levels** (M5 and M15 UOEDT, baseline, LOEDT: `{name, tf, price, role}`)                             | the `levels` array of the MCD1, MCD2 and MCD3 readings in `mcd_outputs.envelope` for the zone's `cycle_slot`, VALID or CAUTIONARY sensors only (MCD3 repeats the others: de-duplicated) | none                                   | the SHA-256 of `envelope_json` equals `envelope_sha256`, and the row's `inputs_sha256` equals the zone reading's |
| **Support and resistance** (`sr_1` to `sr_16`, nulls skipped; `sr_9` to `sr_16` have the origin `sr2_levels`) | `context_levels` inside the stored cycle bundle, `market_cycle_inputs.bundle_gz` (gzip of the canonical text)                                                                           | 90 days (a setup is long void by then) | `inputs_sha256` of the row equals the SHA-256 of the unzipped text, and `bundle_bytes` equals its length         |

Two consequences, both stated so that Davin can overrule them (D6). First, **the raw channel columns of the bundle's bars are not the right source**, although "the bundle" is the natural phrase: the zone builder takes channel levels from sensor readings that are usable, so reading the bars would offer a stop behind a level of a sensor that was INVALID or STALE in that cycle. Second, the access is a TypeScript twin of two small Python functions (the availability rule, de-duplication, the `sr` naming), so it is held to the Python by a corpus: for every one of the 31 stored golden zones whose basis is `LEVEL`, the nearest level beyond the zone on the stop side, $0.50 further out, must equal the zone's stored `invalidation_price` (for the others, the result must be the exact $13 rule), and the nearest level beyond the reference price on the profit side must equal `next_opposing_price`.

I ran the real Python assembly on the stored 18 Sep cycle to see what the options are. The structure set is M5 UOEDT 4384.23, M5 baseline 4367.20, M5 LOEDT 4350.16, M15 UOEDT 4279.46, M15 baseline 4214.17, M15 LOEDT 4126.24, and `sr_6` 4398.29, `sr_5` 4386.20, `sr_1` 4369.57, `sr_2` 4350.92, `sr_3` 4334.56. For Z1 (LONG, entry 4367.20) the stop options at Min SLD $13, each $0.50 beyond its level, are:

| Level (below the entry) | Stop price | Stop distance                                 |
| ----------------------- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| M15 `sr_2` 4350.92      | 4350.42    | $16.78 (this is the stored zone invalidation) |
| M5 LOEDT 4350.16        | 4349.66    | $17.54                                        |
| M15 `sr_3` 4334.56      | 4334.06    | $33.14                                        |
| M15 UOEDT 4279.46       | 4278.96    | $88.24                                        |
| M15 baseline 4214.17    | 4213.67    | $153.53                                       |
| M15 LOEDT 4126.24       | 4125.74    | $241.46                                       |

So the architecture's two-option example becomes six (D7 decides how many the modal shows and which one is pre-selected). When the zone's own basis is `MINIMUM_STOP` or `NO_LEVEL` its stored invalidation is exactly $13 from the reference with no structure behind it (ADR-089 (a)), which 6.5 does not call a structural level; D7 decides how that case is shown. Degradation is explicit: if the readings or the bundle are missing, expired or fail their hash, Engine 4 offers only the zone's own stored invalidation and says that the other levels could not be read. It never guesses a level.

## 4. Design in one picture

- **Where.** TypeScript in the monolith, `lib/engine4/` (pure functions, no I/O, no floating point in the money maths) plus `lib/engine4/read/` (thin readers over `marketPrisma`). Routes under `app/api/engine4/`, components under `components/report2/`.
- **In.** The profile (8 metrics), the SYN reading and the chosen zone, the sensors' stored readings and the stored bundle of the zone's cycle, the newest cycle's status and price, the newest `symbol_specs` row, `economic_events`, the trader's inputs.
- **Out.** The modal definition (direction, pills = zone reference prices, stop options, pre-set risk with reasons, bounds); the validated setup; three sized scenarios with declared and actual risk; the badge; Report 2 from a fixed template; a consent record. No model writes a number.
- **Stored.** Three tables in the user schema: the current profile, its append-only history, and the append-only consent record (D10). Migration file only, not applied.
- **Off by default.** One flag (proposal: `ENGINE4_REPORT2_ENABLED`, default off); nothing deployed; nothing applied. Every part ends with tests green and a hand-off.
