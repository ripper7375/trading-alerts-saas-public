## 6. Decisions needed from Davin, with the options seen

The plan's D1 to D17 and the eight readings of part 1 are settled. These are the points where the plan or the architecture was silent. **The recommendation is the default, and it is what is built.**

1. **Where a stop option comes from.** D7 (a): "all structural levels at or beyond the trader's Min SLD on the stop side". Built: every structure level strictly on the stop side of the CHOSEN entry (the zone's price, another pill, or a custom entry), in cents, with a stop $0.50 beyond it. This is wider than the zone's own rule, which looks past the zone's low: with a small Min SLD a level inside the zone, below the reference price, is a nearer option than the zone's invalidation (the test `the option beside the invalidation is found by its price` shows it). _Recommend as built._
2. **"At or beyond Min SLD" is measured on the STOP, not on the level.** A level 12.50 away qualifies at a Min SLD of $13 (its stop, $0.50 beyond, is exactly $13 away), exactly as the builder tests its own invalidation. The other reading (the level itself 13 away) would drop the case the builder keeps. _Recommend as built._
3. **The pre-selection (D7 (c)) in detail.** The stored invalidation is measured from the entry the trader chose, not from the zone's reference price. A LEVEL invalidation at least Min SLD away is pre-selected and linked to its option; a MINIMUM*STOP or NO_LEVEL invalidation at least Min SLD away is pre-selected as the "minimum stop distance, no structure behind it" and never listed as a structural level (even when the MINIMUM_STOP zone names the level that was too close); otherwise the nearest option; with no option, only a custom stop. \_Recommend as built.*
4. **The cells of the badge table that 6.5 leaves open (ADR-063).** (a) A **conflict** is MCD3 reporting `MCD3_NON_CONSOLIDATED_TREND_CONFLICT`. (b) **"With the trend on M5 and M15"** is a WITH*TREND reading in which both channel sensors, MCD1 (M15) and MCD2 (M5), vote the reading's own direction. (c) The table names the HIGHEST badge each row allows (counter-trend or conflict: Conservative; with the trend on one timeframe: Normal; on both: Aggressive); the badge goes to the highest scenario at or below that cap whose target sits strictly before the level, so with the trend and room for Conservative only, the badge is Conservative. (d) With none at or below the cap that fits there is no badge and the reason says whether nothing fits at all or only scenarios above the cap do. (e) A counter-trend setup whose profile RRR is the 1.50 floor has no Conservative scenario, so it gets no badge. The alternative to (e) is to give the lowest offered scenario the Conservative badge. \_Recommend as built;* note that on the 18 Sep reading the Scalper's row is the conflict row (rule 3s, with the trend, but MCD3 reports the conflict).
5. **Room and badge compare the target's CHART (bid) level with the level's price** (`TargetPlan.targetChartLevel`), so the spread counts: a BUY's target is $0.25 further out at a 25-point spread, and a level between the bare distance and the spread-adjusted one does not clear. With a spread of 0 it is the same as comparing distances. _Recommend as built._
6. **A stored zone that disagrees with the levels read back degrades the stop choices** (reason `ZONE_DISAGREES_WITH_LEVELS`), exactly as an unreadable bundle does. This is an addition to the plan, which lists only a missing, expired or failing-a-hash source. It proves at run time what the corpus proves in the test: that these levels are the ones the zone was made from. It cannot fire on the 525 zones of the corpus; it would fire if the twin ever drifts from the Python, or if a sensor reading were corrected after the zone was made. _Recommend keeping it;_ removing it is deleting the `zoneDisagreement` call in `buildStopChoices`.
7. **The reader is all-or-nothing, and is NOT exported from the barrel.** Any problem leaves `levels` empty ("it never guesses a level"). A sensor with no row is not a problem unless the SYN reading names it (a sensor can be off); an INVALID or STALE reading gives no levels and is not a problem; "expired" means no bundle row and a cycle older than 90 days (a bundle that is still stored is used). The clock is a parameter (`nowSeconds`). `index.ts` exports the reader's TYPES only: a Part 7 component that imports the barrel must not pull in Prisma, `crypto` and `zlib`, so server code imports `@/lib/engine4/read/structure-levels` by path; the exactness guard fails if a pure file imports or re-exports the reader as a value, and a test shows the barrel loads without the database client. _Recommend as built._
8. **The guard was loosened in one place, on purpose.** The pure files may import sibling files only (as in part 1); files under `read/` may also import the parent's files, `@/lib/db/market-prisma`, `crypto` and `zlib`, and nothing else. The no-`Math`, no-`Number`, no-float, no-number-arithmetic rules apply to `read/` as everywhere. _Recommend as built._
9. **`DecimalLike` now admits a `Rational`** (part 1's input type). It always did at run time (`Rational.of` returns it as it is); the type now says so, so exact values can be passed on without a round trip through text. Nothing else in part 1 changed, and its 2,048 tests pass unchanged. _Recommend as built._
10. **Files beyond the plan's list, and one coupling.** I added `zone.ts` (the shape of a stored zone), `helpers/stored.ts`, `structure_worlds.py` with its fixture, and `structure-corpus.test.ts`. The new script imports `world()` from the Python engine's own test module (`mcd_worker.tests.test_zones_invariants`) rather than copying it, so that the random worlds are the ones the builder's own invariant tests use; if that test module is renamed, the script needs a one-line change (and the fixture records the SHA-256 of `zones.py`, so a builder change is noticed). _Recommend as built._

## 7. Problems and surprises the next session must know

- **The $13 floor edge is 12.50 away, not 13.50.** A stop sits $0.50 beyond its level, so a level 12.50 behind the reference price puts the stop at exactly $13.00 (the floor is kept: basis LEVEL); at 12.49 the stop would be 12.99 and the builder raises it to $13 and says MINIMUM_STOP.
