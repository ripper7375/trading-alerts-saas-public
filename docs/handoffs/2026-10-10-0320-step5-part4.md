## 6. Decisions for Davin (readings of mine where the plan or the architecture was silent)

I built each as the default below; say if any is wrong. None touches money or authentication in the sense of non-negotiable 5 (they are the checks that stand in front of the sizing, and the sizing itself is part 1).

1. **`ValidatedSetup` holds the validation, the sizing, the scenarios and the underflow help; the badge is not in it.** It is plain data (strings, booleans, arrays; the Rational figures print as exact decimal text or a fraction), so `JSON.stringify` of it is the "byte-identical" the architecture asks for (`serializeValidatedSetup`). It records what the consent record needs of it: the inputs in canonical text, the pinned cycle, the checks with their codes, the override, the notices, the holding-window warnings, and the versions of the broker row and of the Tier-1 list. The badge needs the synthesis' trend context and the levels beyond the target, and Report 2 is where those meet; it stays a separate call.
2. **The fields.** `entry`, `equity`, `riskPct`, `stopDistance` (dollars from the entry, "$18.50" in chat) and `rrr` (the Normal scenario's target RRR) are all required: a missing one is a code (`ENTRY_MISSING`, `EQUITY_MISSING`, ...), not a default, because the trader confirms each input. `side` and `zoneId` are optional. The equity is checked under check 1 (6.10 has no row for it) and may differ from the profile's (an edit mid-session). Percent signs and thousands separators are not read (`"1.2%"` is `RISK_NOT_A_NUMBER`); the extractor of step 6 normalises.
3. **An entry is a zone price or a custom entry by its VALUE.** A price equal to a zone's reference price (of this reading's side) is that zone, whatever the text looked like; a pick (`zoneId`) with no price typed fills the price in; a pick with a different price typed is a custom entry and the pick is ignored. Two zones at one price go to the better-ranked. A zone price is always legal and skips the bounds of check 0 (the zone builder made it); only a custom entry is held to them. The same value arriving by the modal and by chat is therefore the same setup.
4. **Check 0 looks at the 5% filter before the day range** (the more telling message), **and what is not known is a refusal**: no live price or no closed bar in the last 24 hours refuses a custom entry (never a zone price). Both edges of both bounds are inclusive.
5. **The one-day range.** Closed bars only (a bar is closed when its open time plus five minutes is not after `now`); a bar opened exactly 24 hours ago counts. A bar that cannot be read, or whose high is below its low, makes the whole range unknown rather than being skipped. **There is no minimum number of bars:** after a weekend or a restart the last 24 hours hold few bars, the range is narrow, and only entries close to the price pass. The offer check already refuses MARKET_CLOSED and STALE data, so this is the narrow window after the reopening.
6. **Check 6 is the offer check, run again.** It fails with the codes of the offer's rows 6, 7 and 9 (the synthesis, the data status, the price against the invalidation), with `SETUP_CHANGED` when a refresh is pending, with `PAST_INVALIDATION` when the picked zone is past it (a custom entry is not tied to a zone), and with `DIRECTION_MISMATCH` when a typed side is not the synthesis' (BUY, SELL, LONG or SHORT, upper case; anything else is `SIDE_NOT_RECOGNISED`). The blackout (row 8) belongs to check 5 and the broker figures (row 10) to check 7, so a failure shows once.
7. **Check 7 fails when there are no broker figures** (`NO_BROKER_FIGURES`, with the broker problem as a note) and when the lot is below the minimum (`LOT_BELOW_BROKER_MINIMUM`, with the underflow help and the causes). The help is given the structural stops of THIS entry, so a nearer structural stop can be offered; it never asks for another equity or more leverage.
8. **Statuses.** PASS, FAIL, or SKIPPED (the check needed an input an earlier check refused). The setup is sized, and its scenarios built, only when the entry, risk, stop and RRR are acceptable and there are broker figures; `ok` is true only when all eight pass. A blackout or a stale-data refusal does not stop the sizing: the trader sees the numbers and the reason.
9. **The override** is recorded when the risk chosen is above the pre-set and within Max RPT, with the pre-set, the chosen figure and the reasons; the pre-set itself, anything below it, and anything above Max RPT record nothing.
10. **The modal definition.** A zone past its invalidation stays a pill, flagged `invalidated` (so the order never shifts under the trader); a zone the offer check did not look at is flagged too. A refresh offered keeps the pills and adds the refresh; not offered gives the reasons and nothing else. A custom entry's stop options are computed for ITS price (`customEntryStopChoices`), the nearest pre-selected. The RRR preset is the profile's target, lowered to 2.50 for a counter-trend setup, with `capped` saying so.
11. **No separate Engine 4 version string yet.** The schema id `validated-setup/1` is what a record carries; a release version for the consent record (6.11 "versions: Engine 4") can be added in part 5 when the table is written.
12. **Additive changes to earlier parts:** `OfferResult.direction` and `trendRelation` (part 3's `offer.ts`, tested), `byRank` in `zone.ts`, and the golden loader of the test helpers reading `reference_price`.
13. **Files beyond the plan's list:** `__tests__/lib/engine4/helpers/setup.ts` (the shared starting point of the tests), and `byRank`.

## 7. Problems and surprises

1. **The stored bundles hold closes only.** The M5 bars of the bundle carry `timestamp`, `close` and the channel columns, no `high` or `low`, so the real one-day range of the 18 Sep cycle cannot be rebuilt from the fixtures. The tests use TEST bars built to give the DAY line of architecture 5.5 (high 4391.20, low 4318.75), with a bar still forming and a bar older than 24 hours carrying absurd figures that must be ignored. They are labelled as test data in the helper. The real range comes from `market_data_v6` in production.
2. **"Identical output" is true by construction, and the test's work is the normalisation.** Both paths call the same function, so the content of the test is that `"4367.20"`, `4367.2`, `" 4367.2000 "`, `"16.7800"` and a bare pick all come out as the same canonical text.
3. **A check can be SKIPPED only when another has FAILED.** The cascade is by design (no sizing on numbers that were refused), and a SKIPPED check with no FAIL beside it cannot occur (one mutant that treats SKIPPED as a pass is equivalent for that reason).
4. **The offer check cannot know which zone the entry is** before the validator has read the entry, so check 6 reads the per-zone flags the offer check already returns (`offer.zones`) instead of asking it again with a pick.
5. **My mutation harness crashed once** for a missing entry (no test order for the one file where I had added a function), at 397 of 465; the restart took up the rest, nothing was lost. The files were scanned for CRLF before the copies were made and the copies are checked for it by the harness now.
