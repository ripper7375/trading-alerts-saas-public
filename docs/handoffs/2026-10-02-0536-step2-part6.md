### How the flip works

Every consumer asks the setting about **the slot of the cycle it is working on**: the sensor worker the `cycle-ready` job's slot (`resolveAll(slot)` in process), the monolith and the VPS renderer the newest READY cycle's slot (`GET /api/v1/cycles/current` resolves it there for them). A setting effective from slot T therefore applies to the first cycle at or after T and to no earlier one, for all of them, and a replay of a slot before T gets the source that was active then. Nothing here reads a clock to decide which indicator applies (the one exception is a database with no READY cycle at all, where the answer says `basis: WALL_CLOCK`).
So "at T" means **when the cycle of slot T becomes READY** (about a minute after T), not at the wall-clock instant T; the overlay shows it at its next refresh, within 90 s after the slot.

## 7. Problems and surprises the next session must know

1. **The channel route is implemented twice.** `operation-service` has a port of it (`MIGRATE_MARKET_DATA_CHANNEL`, default off). The monolith route therefore resolves the indicator first and puts it into the forwarded query, leaving operation-service untouched; but operation-service does not know `fractal_edt`, so with that flag on and the fractal EDT active the forwarded call answers 400. Recorded in `waiting-on.md`.
2. **Which slot is "future".** The floor is the later of the wall-clock slot and the newest READY cycle's slot, plus one slot: the current slot cannot be set (a consumer may already have been served it), and a READY cycle ahead of the gateway's clock raises it. The earliest change is therefore the next slot; at most a week ahead; a millisecond timestamp is refused as too far.
3. **Cancelling a scheduled change is writing the old source again for that slot**: two rows for the same (timeframe, slot) are resolved by who wrote last, then by id. Nothing is ever updated or deleted (a source guard and a store with no update or delete keep it so).
4. **The downloaded chart can disagree with the screen after a change.** `/api/chart/download` serves an image rendered on the VPS, whose overlay indicator is the renderer's own until part 7.
5. **No setting is "unknown", never a default.** If the migration's two starting rows are missing, the endpoint reports null and the route answers 503; the defaults live in the migration only.
6. **The admin author is the session's**: `email (id)`, capped at 200 characters; a `setBy` in the request is refused. The gateway trusts the service key and records what it is told; it cannot check who the admin is.
7. **No UI text was added** (no `.tsx` markup changed), so the i18n coverage rules do not apply; the route's 503 message is English like the existing 403 upsell message it sits beside, shown as is by the hook.
8. **`ReaderCycle` grew three timing fields** (part 5's reader); nothing else of the readers changed.
