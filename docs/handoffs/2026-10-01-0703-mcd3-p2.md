## 7. Problems and surprises the next session must know

1. **`T_EDT` counts the forming bar (new evidence).** The band columns (UOEDT, LOEDT, baseline) are populated on exactly `T_EDT` rows ending at the still-open bar, on 7 of 7 real channels in v1 and v4 (M5 `best_fit_a` 755, `fractal` 336, `cherry_a` 1134, `fractal` 410; M15 `non_b` 1808 and 2035, `non_a` 968). On closed bars the channel has `T_EDT − 1` bars. A window of `T_EDT` closed bars reads a null on its oldest bar and would end every real cycle as `DISCONTINUITY`; the legacy hid this by skipping nulls. The spec uses `T_EDT − 1` (Q1, parameter `t_edt_open_bar_rows`). This is **new evidence for the open waiting-on item** ("the statistics' fit windows may include the still-open bar"), not a proof: whether the fit used the forming bar's price still needs the MQL5 source.
2. **The committed MCD2 has a latent window defect.** Its window is `max(48, min(T_EDT, 288))` closed M5 bars, so for an M5 channel with `T_EDT` between 49 and 288 it reaches one bar before the channel exists, and MCD2 ends INVALID + `DISCONTINUITY`. Simulated on v1 (bands removed beyond `T_EDT − 1` rows): `T_EDT` 289 and above VALID, 288 and below INVALID. MCD1 has the same at `T_EDT` ≤ 96. **No replica triggers it** (the smallest real `T_EDT` is 314, v3 `fractal`), but live fractal channels can be short, and an MCD2 INVALID reaches MCD3 as `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE:MCD2`. Outside this task and not fixed: a change to MCD2 or MCD1 is task P7 (a PATCH: no fixture changes). Recorded in `waiting-on.md`.
3. **A real consolidated trend exists: replica v3** (slot 2026-09-28T14:15Z), M15 `non_b` + M5 `cherry_a`: both DOWN, 973 of 1037 closed M5 bars nested (93.83), current bar engulfed, `MCD3_BEAR_BOTTOM`.
