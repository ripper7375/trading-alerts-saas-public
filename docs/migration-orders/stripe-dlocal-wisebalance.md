# STRIPE / DLOCAL -------> WISE BALANCE

Let me ask you further about Wise Balance. Is it possible to connect Wise Balance with Stripe and/or dLocal so that money (USD) received from customers (both Stripe and dLocal) could directly transfered to Wise Balance (in USD). If yes, how about transaction costs of these ?

======================================

**YES! 100% YES! This is the ultimate, most cost-effective setup used by global SaaS companies!** 🚀

Here is how it works, how to set it up, and the complete fee breakdown:

---

### 1. Connecting Stripe to your Wise USD Balance

Your Wise Business account gives you **real US Bank Account details** (ACH Routing Number & Account Number).

#### How to set it up:

1. Log into **Stripe Dashboard** → **Settings** → **Bank accounts and scheduling**.
2. Click **Add bank account**.
3. Enter your Wise USD account details (ACH Routing Number & Account Number).

#### Transaction Costs for Stripe → Wise:

- **Stripe USD Payout Fee**: **$0.00 (100% FREE)**
- **Wise Receiving ACH Fee**: **$0.00 (100% FREE)**
- **Total Fee**: **$0.00 FREE!**

---

### 2. Connecting dLocal to your Wise USD Balance

dLocal settles merchant earnings in USD directly to your bank account.

#### How to set it up:

- In your dLocal Merchant Portal settlement settings, provide your Wise USD account details (or Wise SWIFT details) for USD settlement.

#### Transaction Costs for dLocal → Wise:

- Standard dLocal merchant settlement agreement fee. Wise charges **$0 fee** for receiving ACH deposits!

---

### 3. Huge Strategic Advantages of this Setup:

1. **ZERO FX Loss on Customer Income**:
   - Customer pays $100 USD → Stripe collects $100 USD → Wise receives $100 USD in your USD balance with **$0 fee**!
2. **Self-Funding Payout Engine**:
   - Your customer subscription revenue automatically fills your Wise USD Balance!
3. **No International SWIFT Wires from Thailand**:
   - You **never** need to make manual SWIFT wires from SCB to fund Wise! The customer income sitting inside Wise automatically funds your affiliate payouts!
4. **Maximum Profitability**:
   - You eliminate foreign exchange conversion fees on incoming revenue completely!
