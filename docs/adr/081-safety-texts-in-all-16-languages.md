# ADR-081: Safety texts in all 16 languages

- **Status:** Settled
- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Section:** 7 · Platform & governance (deck decision 7.12: Languages)
- **Architecture:** [STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md](../STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md) §7.9

## Decision

Safety texts in all 16 before release; RTL tested.

## Alternative not chosen

English fallback.

## Why

A disclaimer or refusal in the wrong language may not protect the trader or DavinTrade; Arabic and Urdu need right-to-left rendering.
