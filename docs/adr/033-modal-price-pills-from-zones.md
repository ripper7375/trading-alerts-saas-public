# ADR-033: Modal price pills from zones

- **Status:** Settled
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Section:** 3 · Synthesis & entry zones (deck decision 3.9: The modal’s five prices)
- **Architecture:** [STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md](../STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md) §3.6

## Decision

One reference price per zone, best first; fewer if fewer qualify.

## Alternative not chosen

A 5-step ladder.

## Why

Prices tied to real levels instead of an evenly spaced $2.50 ladder; no filler prices when fewer zones qualify.
