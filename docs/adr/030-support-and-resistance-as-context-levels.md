# ADR-030: Support and resistance as context levels

- **Status:** Settled
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Section:** 3 · Synthesis & entry zones (deck decision 3.6: Levels beyond the MCDs)
- **Architecture:** [STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md](../STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md) §3.6

## Decision

sr_1–sr_16 as context levels until an S&R sensor exists.

## Alternative not chosen

MCD channel levels only.

## Why

MCDs output channel lines only; the 16 support & resistance levels give zones and targets something to aim at until an S&R sensor exists.
