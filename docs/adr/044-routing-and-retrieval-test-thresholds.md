# ADR-044: Routing and retrieval test thresholds

- **Status:** Settled
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Section:** 4 · Intake, routing & knowledge (deck decision 4.9: Test thresholds)
- **Architecture:** [STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md](../STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md) §4.8

## Decision

Routing ≥ 90% · recall@4 ≥ 0.8 · languages within 5 points.

## Alternative not chosen

Set after first run.

## Why

Starting bars that make routing and retrieval quality measurable in every language; to raise as the corpus matures.
