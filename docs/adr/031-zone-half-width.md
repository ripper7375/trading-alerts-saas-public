# ADR-031: Zone half-width

- **Status:** Settled
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Section:** 3 · Synthesis & entry zones (deck decision 3.7: Zone geometry)
- **Architecture:** [STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md](../STACK-D-ARCHITECTURE.md) §3.6

## Decision

Half-width 10% of the source channel width; overlaps merge.

## Alternative not chosen

ATR-based width.

## Why

The width scales with the channel the level comes from; overlapping zones mark confluence.
